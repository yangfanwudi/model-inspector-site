About this site

Model Inspector is a browser-based WebGL tool built with Three.js and Vite: drag in any .glb/.gltf, inspect it under a three-point lighting rig on a procedural pedestal, switch to PBR inspection mode, and export 4K PNGs with transparent backgrounds. Zero external assets; it works offline.
